
# Funds Settlement

Some merchants wish that collected fund can be settled every so often. This section describes how the merchant can initiate a funds settlement request to the finance team for handling.

**Step 1: Click "Settlement Requests" Menu.**

**Step 2: Click "New Settlement Request" Button.**

<figure><img src="https://388154480-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FJJeVyAFgsYo2FQfi2OB0%2Fuploads%2FSjm5j1He4oJn28kqpB5V%2FCreate%20New%20Settlement%20Request%20for%20%2490000%20Transaction%20-%20Step%202.png?alt=media&amp;token=1ca51b0b-4786-4af4-8fc4-ee1349a2e709" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3: Click Dropdown, Select Merchant Account.**

**Step 4: Click Dropdown, Select Currency.**

<figure><img src="https://388154480-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FJJeVyAFgsYo2FQfi2OB0%2Fuploads%2FIcmST1Ni6xZTd8BXdQiM%2FCreate%20New%20Settlement%20Request%20for%20%2490000%20Transaction%20-%20Step%204.png?alt=media&amp;token=f1b5ddf2-47be-4013-9958-bb01eab66c42" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 5: Click Dropdown, Select Source Wallet.**

<figure><img src="https://388154480-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FJJeVyAFgsYo2FQfi2OB0%2Fuploads%2FuIUPjN4yFsvOrWaIlsCU%2FCreate%20New%20Settlement%20Request%20for%20%2490000%20Transaction%20-%20Step%205.png?alt=media&amp;token=c951eb25-bb4e-42d8-9a5c-73638372bac1" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 6: Click the "Request Amount (Value Required)" field. Enter Settlement Amount.**

<figure><img src="https://388154480-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FJJeVyAFgsYo2FQfi2OB0%2Fuploads%2FzBEYibwWWqQ3DqkcdmFq%2FCreate%20New%20Settlement%20Request%20for%20%2490000%20Transaction%20-%20Step%206.png?alt=media&amp;token=06115b50-859e-48a0-8c5b-3d506e4220ba" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 7: Select Dropdown, Select Merchant Account Bank.**

<figure><img src="https://388154480-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FJJeVyAFgsYo2FQfi2OB0%2Fuploads%2Fafi4A8NNqlD56kXdYBDq%2FCreate%20New%20Settlement%20Request%20for%20%2490000%20Transaction%20-%20Step%207.png?alt=media&amp;token=7e3dd9e4-141a-4795-b90f-7050e204e2db" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 8: Click "Create " Button.**

<figure><img src="https://388154480-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FJJeVyAFgsYo2FQfi2OB0%2Fuploads%2FHJxWNgvK3oNvRx7jMoQM%2FCreate%20New%20Settlement%20Request%20for%20%2490000%20Transaction%20-%20Step%208.png?alt=media&amp;token=b7a095d9-0e0b-4028-8155-1ccde8cdc713" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 9: On the popup window, Click "Submit for Processing".**

<figure><img src="https://388154480-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FJJeVyAFgsYo2FQfi2OB0%2Fuploads%2F79JZbc5yCyHxixTZtkDw%2FCreate%20New%20Settlement%20Request%20for%20%2490000%20Transaction%20-%20Step%209.png?alt=media&amp;token=82c9e052-5803-4f87-bad6-aca98c7394bd" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 10: Once you have Confirmed and Submitted for Processing, Click "Cancel" Button to return to the Settlement Requests Report.**
